About the game

One sentence: Build a home at the edge of the world in a warm settlement-building adventure shaped by cultivation, community, and discovery.

Far beyond the familiar world, a quiet valley is waiting. Gather its resources, shape your settlement, and build a place worth calling home.

Key features

  • Build and grow a settlement through deliberate choices.
  • Cultivate the land and adapt to a changing valley.
  • Explore old roads and discover stories beyond the gate.
  • Play on desktop or mobile, with platform-specific experiences planned.
