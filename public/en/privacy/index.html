Privacy

A plain-language outline of the information this website will use. This draft must be completed with Pangea’s legal identity and reviewed before production launch.

Draft updated: July 2026 · Not final legal text

Before launch: add the legal company name, business location, privacy contact, retention periods, applicable legal bases, and any jurisdiction-specific disclosures.

Information you provide

When you subscribe for release updates, we collect your email address and consent choice. When you contact us or request support, we collect the fields shown in the relevant form, such as your reply email, subject, message, platform, and optional name.

How we use it

We use newsletter information to send the updates you requested. We use contact and support information to review, route, and answer your inquiry, protect our services from abuse, and maintain an appropriate operational record.

Service providers

The website is planned to run on Microsoft Azure. Email subscriptions and form notifications are planned to use Twilio SendGrid. These providers may process information on Pangea Games’ behalf under their respective service agreements.

Retention and security

We will retain personal information only as long as needed for the stated purpose, legal obligations, and reasonable operational records. Access will be limited, credentials will remain server-side, and form bodies will not be intentionally written to application telemetry.

Your choices

Newsletter messages will include an unsubscribe method. You may contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion rights that apply to your information. The final privacy contact will be published here before launch.

International use

The website is intended for an international audience. The final policy will describe relevant international transfers and regional rights once Pangea’s legal entity and operating location are confirmed.

Contact

Until a dedicated privacy address is published, use the contact form and select “Privacy.”
